Privacy Policy

CineStream · com.aistudio.cinestream.vqdpmr

Effective 12 August 2026 Last updated 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CineStream (“we”, “our” or “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you install and use our Android application (the “App”). By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you give us

CineStream requires no account, sign-in or registration. We do not ask for and do not store your name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address or any other directly identifying information on servers operated by us.

B. Information collected automatically

When you use the App, the following technical data may be processed on your device or by the third-party services listed in section 3:

  • Device information — device model, Android version, screen size, system language and approximate region derived from the store/market you installed from.
  • App usage information — screens opened, playback state, interaction events and crash/diagnostic logs used to keep playback stable.
  • Advertising identifier — the resettable Google Advertising ID (GAID) used by Google AdMob to serve and measure advertisements.
  • Media metadata requests — the titles you search for are sent to the metadata and media providers described in section 3 so they can return results. These requests do not include your identity.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, your photos, your files, your calendars or your messages.

C. Information stored only on your device

Your watchlist, favourites, watch history, search history, playback position, subtitle and player preferences, and downloaded media files are stored in the App’s private sandbox on your device. They are never uploaded to a server operated by us. Uninstalling the App removes them, subject to any files you saved to shared storage yourself.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain and optimise video playback, streaming, downloads and subtitles.
  • Remember your preferences and resume what you were watching.
  • Detect crashes, network errors and playback faults so we can fix them.
  • Display advertising through Google AdMob that keeps the App free to use.
  • Deliver optional in-app update notifications.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles on our own systems; advertising profiling is performed by Google under its own policies.

4. Permissions We Request

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — fetch metadata, streams, subtitles and updates.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and related notification permissions — keep downloads and playback running reliably when the App is not in the foreground.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show download progress and playback controls.
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — play media files you choose from your device.
  • WAKE_LOCK — prevent the device sleeping during playback.
  • AD_ID — allow Google AdMob to access the advertising identifier.

Permissions are requested only when the related feature is used and can be revoked at any time in Android system settings.

5. Retention and Deletion

Because we do not operate accounts or store your data on our servers, there is no server-side copy of your information to delete. To remove all locally stored data you can:

  • Clear the App’s storage from Settings → Apps → CineStream → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the App.

Third-party providers retain data according to their own retention policies. To reset advertising personalisation, use Settings → Google → Ads on your device and choose “Delete advertising ID” or opt out of personalised ads.

6. Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS where the provider supports it. Data on your device is protected by Android’s application sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

CineStream is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will act promptly to remove it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to personalised advertising. Because we hold no account data about you, most requests can be satisfied on your device using the steps in section 5. You may still contact us with any request and we will respond within the period required by law.

9. International Transfers

Third-party providers listed in section 3 may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Those providers are responsible for the safeguards they apply to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date, and where appropriate announced in the App or in the store listing. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
